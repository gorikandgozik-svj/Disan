# Reference-derived art direction (no third-party images distributed)

The originating brief requested premium UI and photographic realism. Reference photographs and concept mockups are **not** distributed, as their copyright and model/brand licensing status is unknown. The following is an **abstract design-quality specification**, not evidence of implementation.

## UI qualities

- Dark premium dashboards: graphite, carefully selected warm metal-like highlights, modular composition, legible analytics, subtle depth.
- Tactile/glass smart-home controls: frosted surfaces, readable labels, restrained glow, touch states and controls that really work.
- Photo-led travel interfaces: scenic imagery only when legitimately licensed, strongly differentiated CTA, concise typography.
- Precision dashboards: meaningful whitespace, grid alignment, keyboard accessible interactive controls and accurate actual data.
- Illustrative scenes: layered foreground/midground/background designed for motion rather than one flattened screenshot.

## 3D / game art targets

- Dense urban skyline and human-scale historic settlements with real roads, varied rooftops, landscape ecology and plausible lighting.
- Winter landscapes with believable vegetation, accumulated snow, low-angle sunlight and physically coherent dusk colors.
- Sports-car/sedan/coupe bodywork and realistic aircraft/engines: multi-view references, exact silhouette, trim and internal mechanisms only when authoritative licensed sources available.
- Real-time: LOD, instancing, culling, PBR materials, performance budgets and measured p95 frame times.

## Honest acceptance gates

1. A 2D image is **not** 3D geometry; a screenshot placed as a background is **not** a functional UI.
2. Each asset identifies its actual tier (`blockout`, `game-ready`, `simulator-grade`, `cinematic`) based on validated artifacts, not the requested tier.
3. Report real source paths, export formats, browser/engine screenshots, errors and limitations.
4. Compare exact-sized screenshots where possible; pixel difference is not a semantic aesthetic score.
5. No claim that photographic realism or Android 60 FPS has been verified until the underlying target and device measurements exist.
